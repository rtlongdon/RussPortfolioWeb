About Russ Longdon

Design engineer, experimental aircraft junkie, and avionics developer.

Background

I'm a Senior Electrical Engineer with over 20 years of experience designing power electronics, embedded systems, and automated test equipment across renewable energy, marine, and emergency vehicle markets. I hold a U.S. patent for charger control architecture and have led full product lifecycle development from concept through production.

I'm a hands-on generalist engineer — equally comfortable working on power electronics, firmware, PCB design, mechanical engineering, and software development. I also hold FAA Commercial Pilot and Certified Flight Instructor certifications, which inform but don't limit my engineering interests.

Technical Capabilities

My background spans multiple engineering disciplines:

  • Power Electronics & Embedded Systems — AC/DC, DC/DC, PFC, PWM design; C, Assembly, Python firmware; ARM Cortex M0/M3/M4, STM32, TI MSP430/MSPM0, MicroChip PIC, ESP32, AVR, Atmel SAM, Renesas Greenpak
  • PCB & Mechanical Design — Altium Designer, KiCad, OrCad, SolidWorks, Fusion 360 for everything from sheet metal to plastic enclosures
  • EMI/EMC Compliance — UL, CE, ISO 7637-2 / 16750-2 standards; transient protection and regulatory compliance
  • Automated Test & Manufacturing — Production test fixtures, burn-in systems, mechanical fixturing; offshore manufacturing collaboration
  • Mobile & Desktop Software (Learning/Hobby) — Android Studio, Kotlin, Java; self-directed learning through building working applications
  • CNC Fabrication & Machining — Custom CNC router design and operation for personal fabrication projects

Philosophy

I believe in building things that work. Whether it's power electronics, firmware, software, or a CNC machine — I prototype, test, iterate, and refine until it's solid. Real-world use is the best teacher. I stay current across multiple disciplines because problems rarely fit into a single specialty, and versatility beats narrow expertise.
