About Russ Longdon

Software engineer, experimental aircraft builder, and avionics developer.

Background

I'm a Senior Electrical Engineer with over 20 years of experience designing power electronics, embedded systems, and automated test equipment for renewable energy, marine, and emergency vehicle markets. I hold a U.S. patent for charger control architecture and have led full product lifecycle development from concept through production.

As a Licensed FAA Commercial Pilot and Certified Flight Instructor, I bring practical aviation experience to every project. I'm currently pursuing self-directed software development focused on aviation-related Android applications for experimental aircraft operations.

Technical Foundation

My background spans:

  • Power Electronics & Embedded Systems — AC/DC, DC/DC, PFC, PWM design; C, Assembly, Python firmware; ARM Cortex M3/M4 microcontrollers
  • EMI/EMC Compliance — UL, CE, ISO 7637-2 / 16750-2 standards; transient protection design
  • PCB & Mechanical Design — Altium Designer, KiCad, SolidWorks, Fusion 360
  • Automated Test Fixtures — Production and burn-in systems, mechanical test fixturing
  • Manufacturing & Offshore Collaboration — Contract manufacturer partnerships and product transfer to volume production

Current Focus

My recent transition into self-directed Android application development leverages decades of embedded systems expertise to build aviation software for experimental aircraft. My work spans:

  • Android Avionics Apps — Moving maps, flight planning, approach training, and secondary flight displays integrated with Dynon SkyView avionics
  • Embedded Hardware & Firmware — Sensor-fusion attitude systems, proportional control interfaces, motor controllers, and wireless modules for cockpit integration
  • Data Pipelines — Offline map generation, chart processing, and airspace/airport data from FAA sources
  • CNC Fabrication — Custom-built CNC router for personal engineering projects and aircraft components

Philosophy

Everything I build starts with a real need in the aircraft. I prototype in the field, test in flight, and iterate based on what I learn. The result is software and hardware that's battle-tested before it's released — combining decades of engineering rigor with practical aviation experience.

Open to Discussion

If you're interested in experimental aviation, avionics development, or sensor fusion systems, I'm always happy to discuss ideas and approaches. These projects represent years of learning from the aviation community, and I'm committed to sharing what I've discovered.
