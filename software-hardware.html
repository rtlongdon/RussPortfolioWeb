A running collection of work spanning Android avionics apps, embedded hardware, and CNC builds — mostly built for experimental aircraft, some built for the shop.

SkyView App Suite

A companion suite of apps built around Dynon SkyView avionics — moving maps, approach training, secondary flight displays, and engine monitoring, sharing a live SkyView data feed — plus the offline pipeline that builds GreatEFB's map data. This project is my sandbox for learning Android development and exploring human-AI collaboration. I brought domain expertise in hardware and embedded systems but started from scratch with Kotlin and Android frameworks. The partnership evolved from the AI providing heavy guidance and detailed walkthroughs early on to something closer to 50/50 collaboration — I now direct the technical decisions and architecture while using AI as a collaborative workhorse to accelerate implementation.
