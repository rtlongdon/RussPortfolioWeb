OutBack FLEXnet DC

A networked DC system monitor that reports battery state of charge, charging status, and real-time DC production and consumption, with up to 128 days of logged history.

My role: Principal electrical engineer, and also handled the mechanical design and integration. We were a startup, so everyone wore a lot of different hats.

Marine & Vehicle

Products developed for Blue Sea Systems.

🔌

Blue Sea Systems P12 Battery Charger

A 12V, three-output, four-stage battery charger (25A and 40A versions) with a universal AC input and charge profiles for flooded, gel, TPPL, and AGM batteries.

My role: Principal power electronics designer, and author of all the firmware. Also principal in developing the automated programming and test fixturing for production, plus the burn-in fixturing, and led the three-person test and burn-in team.

Power electronics Firmware Test & burn-in
🎮

Blue Sea Systems EV Battery Charger Display

A daylight-readable, IP66-rated OLED display that pairs with the P12 charger to show battery voltage, charge stage, and faults, or voltage for up to three battery banks on its own.

My role: Principal electrical engineer. Designed the fully automated production programming and test fixturing.

Electrical design Production test
🚒

Blue Sea Systems Sure Eject

An automatic shore-power plug ejector for emergency vehicles. A motor-driven mechanism ejects the power cord when the vehicle's ignition is turned on, preventing a driver from pulling away while still plugged in, and it retries if the first ejection fails.

My role: Designed the electrical braking system, and took the next-generation, higher-powered design through prototype.

Emergency vehicles Electromechanical

Test Equipment & Systems

Production test rigs and system-level designs.

🔨

Alternator Spin Fixture

A fixture that spins an alternator so an alternator clamp can be tested. The clamp removes residual output caused by magnetic remanence, and the fixture verifies the alternator is truly shut down. Built for Blue Sea Systems.

My role: Designed, fabricated, and tested the fixture and its controls.

Test fixturing Alternators
📦

12V Air Brake Compressor Production Test Rig

A final production test rig for Blue Sea Systems' 12V air brake compressor, which keeps an air brake system charged alongside an engine-driven compressor. The rig uses a small pneumatic storage system and a sensor system to automate regulator-setting and leak testing before shipment.

My role: Sole designer of the rig, a ground-up electro-mechanical design.

Production test 12V
⚓

Hybrid Marine Power Interlock

A charging and battery system interlock that allows safe transfer between power sources in a hybrid marine propulsion and house power system.

My role: Designed the interlock as part of an R&D group researching hybrid, interlinked power sources on marine vessels.
