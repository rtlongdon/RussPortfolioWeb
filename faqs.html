FAQs

What problems do your projects solve?

With the exception of Approach Trainer, none of these ideas are unique. They are my variants of what I think are improvements created while learning new skills.

Then why do all this work?

Because although I can buy most of this capability, that doesn't teach me how it works. Everything I do is to learn and get smarter every day! The smarter I get, the dumber I realize I am. Then I have to go learn some more stuff! ;)

Is there anything you won't mess with?

Things that can eat me, high explosives and squirrel suiting.

Do you use your apps and hardware in the airplane?

Every time I fly, yes. My RV-12ULS (ELSA) is just my airplane, and whatever I'm building rides along with me. If it's useful, it stays. If it isn't, it goes back on the bench. Any data that gets recorded along the way is incidental to normal, fun flying, just like I use the data my Dynon SkyView records to troubleshoot and learn. I'm not running a test program. I'm a pilot using my own gear, and I like to see how it works in the real world. It's really just an excuse to go flying.
