Approach Trainer Companion

A physical BLE knob built on ESP32, giving Approach Trainer tactile altitude bug and baro control — without needing Dynon SkyView present.

Bench validated

Living portfolio — everything subject to change.

What it does

Approach Trainer normally draws its altitude and baro data from Dynon SkyView. This companion knob supplies that same input directly from its own onboard barometric sensor over Bluetooth Low Energy, so Approach Trainer can be used standalone — on the bench, or in any aircraft, with no SkyView connection required — while still giving a physical, panel-like feel instead of touchscreen taps.

Highlights

  • Altitude knob with integrated barometric sensor, bench-validated over BLE
  • Pairs directly with Approach Trainer, removing the dependency on a live SkyView connection
  • Push-button input for mode switching
  • Second knob (OBS/course select) planned, not yet wired

Why a separate altitude sensor?

Aircraft altimeters read pressure from the static source on the fuselage, but airflow around the aircraft distorts that pressure reading at different speeds and attitudes — this is static source error. The barometric sensor inside the Companion knob reads true ambient pressure directly, unaffected by the aircraft's airflow. By trimming the Companion's altitude against your aircraft's altimeter, you can null out your aircraft's static error and give Approach Trainer accurate reference altitude.
