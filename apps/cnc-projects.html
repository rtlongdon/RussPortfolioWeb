8mm Plug Lead Wire Pass-Throughs

Precision-machined electrical pass-throughs fabricated from G-7 Garolite, a high-temperature composite rated to 425°F. Designed for aircraft ignition harnesses (Continental and Lycoming engines) but applicable to automotive, marine, and other high-temperature electrical applications requiring reliable insulation and mechanical support.

Each pass-through features tight tolerances for a clean fit, smooth bore for wire routing, and durable material that resists thermal cycling and chemical exposure.

MaterialG-7 Garolite
Max operating temp425°F
Primary applicationAircraft ignition harnesses (various sizes available)
