ESP32 Knob

A physical BLE knob peripheral built on ESP32, giving tactile control of altitude bug and baro setting to the tablet app suite.

Bench validated

What it does

A standalone hardware knob that pairs to the tablet apps over Bluetooth Low Energy, giving a physical, panel-like feel for adjusting altitude and barometric pressure instead of touchscreen taps.

Highlights

  • Altitude knob with integrated barometric sensor, bench-validated over BLE
  • Push-button input for mode switching
  • Second knob (OBS/course select) planned, not yet wired

Status

Hardware has arrived and the altitude/baro/BLE path is validated on the bench. Next step is wiring up the OBS knob.

PlatformESP32
LinkBluetooth Low Energy
