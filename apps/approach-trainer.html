Approach Trainer

A synthetic approach procedure trainer integrated into the SkyView Android app ecosystem. Uses live avionics data from Dynon SkyView to compute real-time approach guidance to user-defined practice points.

Flight tested

What it does

Approach Trainer lets a pilot fly synthetic instrument approach procedures anywhere, without needing to be near a real published approach. It reads live GPS and attitude data from the SkyView avionics stream and computes course, glidepath, and timing guidance to a practice procedure defined by the pilot.

Highlights

  • Real-time approach guidance computed from live SkyView GPS/ADAHRS data
  • Renders standard IFR approach plate symbology for visual realism
  • Flight tested in the actual aircraft, with results tracked over multiple sessions
  • Shares the SkyView UDP data stream with sibling apps (GreatEFB, EFISDisplay, SFD_Android) running on the same tablet
