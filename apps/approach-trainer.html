Approach Trainer

Realtime approach practice using live avionics data from Dynon SkyView or the Approach Trainer Companion Bluetooth module. Learn on simplified approach plates with guidance to your own practice procedures.

What it does

Approach Trainer lets a pilot practice synthetic instrument approach procedures anywhere. Define a custom approach procedure, then fly it using live GPS and altitude data — either from Dynon SkyView, or from your tablet's built-in GPS paired with the Approach Trainer Companion module for altitude. The app computes real-time guidance (course, glidepath, timing) and displays it on a simplified approach plate designed for learning and practice.

Demo

Highlights

  • Real-time approach guidance from Dynon SkyView or Approach Trainer Companion Bluetooth module
  • Simplified approach plates designed for learning and practice (not a full chart rendering)
  • Define custom practice procedures anywhere — no published approaches needed
  • Flight tested in the actual aircraft with results tracked over multiple sessions
  • When paired with SkyView, shares the UDP data stream with sibling apps (GreatEFB, EFISDisplay, SFD_Android) running on the same tablet

Safety

VFR practice only, with a safety pilot. Approach Trainer is a training tool for practicing procedures in VFR conditions. It is not approved for use in IMC (Instrument Meteorological Conditions). Always maintain visual reference and fly with a safety pilot when using this app.
