GreatEFB

An Android tablet EFB (Electronic Flight Bag) moving map application for experimental aircraft equipped with Dynon SkyView avionics.

Flight tested

Living portfolio — everything subject to change.

What it does

GreatEFB is a full-featured electronic flight bag that displays a moving map with airspace and airport overlays. It optionally integrates with Dynon SkyView over WiFi for live GPS and flight data, but also runs standalone using the tablet's own GPS stream — making it useful whether or not SkyView is present.

Highlights

  • Live moving map driven by SkyView GPS and flight data
  • Airspace and airport overlays, including TFRs
  • Flight plan mode with rubber-banding to adjust a route in flight
  • Weather data integration
  • Portrait-first layout for 7"+ tablets, with landscape/phone support
  • Transparently shares SkyView's UDP streams with up to three sibling apps (Approach Trainer, EFIS Display, SFD Android) running on the same tablet
  • Can also run autonomously from SkyView, falling back to the tablet's own GPS stream
  • Routinely flight tested in the aircraft alongside active development
