GreatEFB

An Android tablet EFB (Electronic Flight Bag) moving map application for experimental aircraft. Runs standalone, and is enriched by a Dynon SkyView datastream when available.

Used in flight

Living portfolio — everything subject to change.

What it does

GreatEFB is a full-featured electronic flight bag that displays a moving map with airspace and airport overlays. It runs standalone using the tablet's own GPS stream, and optionally integrates with Dynon SkyView over WiFi — a SkyView datastream enriches the display and data with GDL90 traffic, NEXRAD weather, and other in-flight data. Useful whether or not SkyView is present.

Highlights

  • Live moving map that runs standalone on the tablet's own GPS; a Dynon SkyView datastream enriches the display with GDL90 traffic, NEXRAD weather, and other in-flight data
  • Airspace and airport overlays, including TFRs
  • Flight plan mode with rubber-banding to adjust a route in flight
  • Weather data integration
  • Portrait-first layout for 7"+ tablets, with landscape/phone support
  • Transparently shares SkyView's UDP streams with up to three sibling apps (Approach Trainer, EFIS Display, SFD Android) running on the same tablet
  • GPS either from the SkyView datastream or the tablet's internal GPS
  • Routinely used in the aircraft on everyday flights alongside active development
