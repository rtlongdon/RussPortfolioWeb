Trim Wheel

A rotary-encoder trim wheel that replaces a momentary trim switch, giving proportional trim control instead of just on/off.

In development

What it does

The aircraft's stock electric trim control is a momentary switch — on or off, no proportional control. This project replaces that switch with a quadrature-encoder wheel that reads position and turn rate, then drives the trim servo motor through an H-bridge at a rate proportional to how fast the wheel is turned — with a dead zone around center and a safety relay cutout so the motor can't run unless the controller commands it.

Highlights

  • Replaces a momentary on/off trim switch with proportional control, driven by how fast the wheel is turned
  • Quadrature rotary encoder senses wheel rotation
  • Motor control state machine with a dead zone, pulse-width drive, and a safety relay that opens by default
  • Custom KiCad PCB ("WheelEtrim") designed and sent out for fabrication
  • Firmware prototyped on TI MSPM0L1306
  • 3D-printed encoder mount/hub and mechanical hardware, with a fit-check mock-up installed in the aircraft floor
