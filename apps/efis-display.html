Engine Monitoring System (EMS)

A Jetpack Compose Android app that listens for UDP broadcast packets from avionics equipment and displays live engine parameters as animated gauges.

Flight tested

Living portfolio — everything subject to change.

What it does

A secondary display that turns a tablet into a dedicated engine monitor, displaying engine parameters from Dynon SkyView via WiFi. Requires a live SkyView connection to operate—it has no standalone capability.

Demo

In-flight view

EMS Active

Gauges displayed

  • RPM, oil pressure, oil temperature, fuel flow, fuel quantity
  • Per-cylinder EGT and CHT bar gauges
  • Configurable yellow/red threshold bands per gauge

Highlights

  • Receives engine data from Dynon SkyView over WiFi (requires active SkyView connection)
  • Routing configurator maps any SkyView data field to the appropriate gauge, accommodating wiring and sensor differences across aircraft
  • Edit and save multiple screen layouts and wiring configurations for different aircraft or setups
  • Configurable yellow/red threshold bands per gauge to match your engine's limits
  • Works in both portrait and landscape orientation
  • Layout designed for quick at-a-glance scanning of engine health
