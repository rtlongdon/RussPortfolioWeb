Engine Monitoring System (EMS)

A Jetpack Compose Android app that listens for UDP broadcast packets from avionics equipment and displays live engine parameters as animated gauges.

Flight tested

What it does

Turns a tablet into a dedicated engine monitor, mirroring the data your primary EFIS shows but with a layout tuned for at-a-glance scanning of engine health.

Gauges displayed

  • RPM, oil pressure, oil temperature, fuel flow, fuel quantity
  • Per-cylinder EGT and CHT bar gauges
  • Configurable yellow/red threshold bands per gauge

Highlights

  • Supports Dynon SkyView EMS UDP protocol (port 49155) out of the box
  • Generic CSV input also supported for testing and non-Dynon setups
  • Threshold values are simple constants, easy to tune to a specific engine's limits
PlatformAndroid (Kotlin)
Data sourceDynon SkyView EMS UDP, or generic CSV
RenderingJetpack Compose
